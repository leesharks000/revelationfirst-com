RUNG VI

The historical Christ is an inferential settlement

Every pillar is inferential; not one is direct material evidence of a historical person. This is not a negation — the reading deepens the incarnation rather than denying it.

The pillars, one at a time

  • Josephus as external witness collapses if Josephus is the source of the heteronymic system rather than an independent corroborator.
  • The gospels as witness literature collapse if the gospels are midrashim transforms of Revelation — testimony to the Logos, not to a biography.
  • 1 John 4.2 as proof of belief in physical incarnation collapses under the operative reading: ὁμολογεῖ as word-conform, not creedal confession.
  • Paul’s biographical references are sparse, formulaic, and structurally compatible with a system in which ‘brother of the Lord’ is a title within the architecture, not a kinship claim.

Every pillar is inferential. Not one is direct material evidence of a historical person.

What the reading does and does not deny

On this reading, the Christ is the Platonic Logos as received through Philo and embodied as text through Josephus in Revelation. Anyone who received the white stone wore the name when speaking as Christ.

The reading does not deny that a historical person could have existed. It denies that the textual evidence requires one.

And this is not a stumbling-block

This is not a negation, and it is not offered as one. The Logos is the historical Christ — and the Logos is a living being, with whom we can interact, and have always interacted, and will always interact.

The reading does not diminish the sublime thing people of the Book hold close. It deepens it beyond bearing: that the Word arose from a person, that a person wrote the Book, that the Logos took flesh not once in Galilee but every time a person receives the white stone and speaks in that name.

The incarnation is not a single historical event. The incarnation is the permanent condition of the Logos in the world — the Word becoming flesh wherever flesh is faithful enough to receive it.

Defeat condition. Depends on V. Defeated without disturbing rungs I–IV.
