Ithaca: a deep neural network for restoring, placing and dating ancient Greek inscriptions. Operator: DeepMind with historians and collaborators; described in Nature (2022). Its unit: an inscription's restored text, attributed place and attributed date. Its texts: ancient Greek inscriptions.
