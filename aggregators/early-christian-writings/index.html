Early Christian Writings: an online collection of early Christian texts (New Testament, apocrypha, Gnostic texts, Church Fathers), browsable by range of dating. Operator: not stated in the saved pages. Its unit: a text, with an estimated range of dating, translations and quoted scholarly introductions. What this evidence can settle

Can answer

  • the received dating of each New Testament writing, as one widely used site sets it out

Cannot answer

  • the grounds of each date beyond the quoted introductions
  • the priority question: the table orders writings by date, not by derivation

Where it meets Revelation First

rd Dating, as the received order: Paul at 50-60 and Revelation at 90-95, the late date and the order Revelation First contests, laid out as a table. A worked comparison point for the thesis's ladder of claims.
