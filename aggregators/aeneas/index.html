Aeneas: an AI model that retrieves textual and contextual parallels for ancient inscriptions, introduced in Nature (2025). Operator: Google DeepMind, co-developed with the University of Nottingham and with researchers at Warwick, Oxford and the Athens University of Economics and Business. Its unit: an inscription with its retrieved parallels. Its texts: Latin inscriptions; stated to be adaptable to other ancient languages, scripts and media, from papyri to coinage.

What this evidence can settle

Can answer

  • which inscriptions share wording, syntax, formulas or provenance with a given inscription

Cannot answer

  • the direction of dependence between parallel texts
  • anything on the New Testament

In the worked questions

  • What can machine dating of manuscripts establish, and what can it not?

Where it meets Revelation First

rd Parallels as evidence: the model retrieves the kind of evidence the midrashim transform argues from (for example Rev 2:10 // Jas 1:12). Retrieval finds parallels; the direction of dependence, which the thesis argues, is not something it settles.
