Bodmer Lab

Papyrology and discoverya digital library of world literature…active
Bodmer Lab: a digital library of world literature from the Bodmer collection, organized in 'constellations', one of them the Bodmer Papyri. Operator: a research and digitization partnership of the University of Geneva's Faculty of Letters and the Fondation Martin Bodmer. Its unit: a digitized document in a constellation; for the papyri, a papyrus or parchment manuscript of the Fondation Martin Bodmer. Its texts: the papyrus and parchment manuscripts of the Fondation Martin Bodmer, among the collection's literature.
