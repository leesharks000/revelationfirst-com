Vesuvius Challenge: an open research competition reading the carbonized Herculaneum scrolls without unrolling them. Operator: Vesuvius Challenge, which describes itself as an open science research organization. Its unit: a scroll, scanned and virtually unwrapped, with ink detected in the scan. What this evidence can settle

Can answer

  • how much of an unopened carbonized scroll can now be read by machine

Cannot answer

  • anything on the New Testament: the Herculaneum library predates it and is not Christian

In the worked questions

  • What can machine dating of manuscripts establish, and what can it not?

Where it meets Revelation First

rd None direct. It shows machine reading recovering text from papyri that cannot be opened, the kind of result that would change what the papyrological record can contain.
