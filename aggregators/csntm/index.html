Revelation First ≠ Revelation Early. The pre-70 date is the old argument (Robinson, 1976; Gentry, 1989); Revelation First is the priority thesis: the rest of the New Testament unfolds from the Apocalypse.

Center for the Study of New Testament Manuscripts: a center that digitizes Greek New Testament manuscripts and publishes their images in a searchable digital manuscript collection. Operator: Center for the Study of New Testament Manuscripts, Plano, Texas; founder and Executive Director Daniel B. Wallace. Its unit: a manuscript, classed as papyrus, majuscule, minuscule or lectionary and dated by century, with its images and a short description. Its texts: Greek New Testament manuscripts, with some Septuagint manuscripts (Rahlfs 961, Genesis on papyrus).

What this evidence can settle

Can answer

  • what a given Greek New Testament manuscript looks like, with its type and century as catalogued
  • where images of P47 (Revelation) and P46 (Paul) can be examined

Cannot answer

  • when Revelation was composed: a manuscript's century dates the copy
  • the order in which the New Testament writings were composed

In the worked questions

  • Which papyri bear on the date of Revelation, and how is each one dated?
  • Which manuscripts can be seen directly online, and which only through an edition?

Where it meets Revelation First

rd Papyrological inversion: P47, the Chester Beatty papyrus of Revelation, and the Pauline P46 are among its imaged papyri. The workstream sets the dates of surviving copies against the received order of composition; the images are where those copies can be read.
