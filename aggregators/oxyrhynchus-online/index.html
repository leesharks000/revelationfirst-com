The Oxyrhynchus Papyri: the collection site of the Oxyrhynchus Papyri, with images, publications, a location list and a virtual exhibition. Operator: the Egypt Exploration Society, whose collection is housed in Oxford's Art, Archaeology and Ancient World Library. Its unit: a text on papyrus or parchment from the Oxyrhynchus excavations. Its texts: literary, documentary and other texts, principally Greek, with Latin, Demotic, Coptic and Arabic.
