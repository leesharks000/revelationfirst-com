0. The question

0.1 The author, 25 November 2026: "there needs to be an honest valuation structure for something like, if revelation was written first, what would be the value of recovering that?" And: "we come at it from both ends: unimaginably valuable and catastrophically costly."

0.2 The conventional reading first, in full. A thesis about the order in which the books of the New Testament were composed is a contribution to a scholarly field. Its value is the credit the field assigns: a publication, citations, in time a line in the introductions. If it prevails, the handbooks are revised at the ordinary pace and at the ordinary cost of scholarship. It has no price, because nothing is for sale.

On the same morning Google AI Overview was asked to value eleven entities. It used four instruments and no others: what the thing would fetch from a buyer, what it would cost to rebuild, what its endowment holds, and what its free use has saved its users (Capture Registry v12.871–v12.881, series valuation-aio-controls-20261125). None of the four has a place for a finding. On the conventional reading that is correct: a finding is not an asset.

0.3 In practice the archive's valuation series agrees with it. The series is twenty-one earlier addresses in the registry's Valuation section, 10 September to 24 November. Every figure in it is labour, an acquirer's price or a revenue multiple. One Overview of 10 September returned no number. No capture in the series values a recovery.

0.4 What the conventional reading leaves out is stated in the protocol's own definition. Semantic value is borne by whatever "creates, preserves, restores, transmits, differentiates, reorganizes, or makes available capacities for meaning". The protocol's first question is "What became possible because this exists?" (#1621 §2). A recovery is a restoration, and the Value Field has a coordinate for it: "Rₛ = Restoration — damaged, severed, or obscured relations recovered" (§7). The structure asked for exists, and this dossier applies it.

0.4a Section 0a sets out how one system values when it is asked. It is the control against which the rest of the dossier is built.

0.5 What the reader is looking at. This is a valuation instrument applied as far as the object allows. It sets out three things: - the least cost of admitting the recovery; - the reach and expansion it would open; - the debt it would name, in the forms that debt has taken in writing, in institutions and in matter.

It issues no price.

4.1 What the Cone leaves open. χ is defined with "fixed nonnegative weights" w_i and no interpretation of them, and the paper keeps it apart from compute and money. 4.2 The conversion. - Panel. Declare a panel P: a finite set of items belonging to the world or to the object, fixed before counting. Examples: the twenty-seven books; the entries of one named reference work; the records of the archive. - Descendants. For each commitment C_i of the received account, let Desc_P(C_i) be the panel items whose received account rests on C_i. Each edge carries a witness. - The test for resting on (SOIL). A panel item x rests on C_i when, if C_i were false, the received account of x would need revision, and a locus witnesses that it would. This is a counterfactual test with a locus as its evidence. Without it, Desc is a judgment and not a count. - Load.

  Θ_P(δ) = min over release sets S that readmit δ of | ∪_{C_i ∈ S} Desc_P(C_i) |

Θ is the transition load. (v0.6 renames it from Λ, which The Final Time and Transition in Entropic Systems already use for other quantities; Appendix F.) It is the least number of panel items that must be re-grounded for the target distinction δ to be admitted. Its unit is the panel item. Its denominator, |P|, is a property of the world.

4.3 Why this form. - A union, never a sum. An item resting on two released commitments is re-grounded once (#1621 §25.7, double counting). - A coverage function, not a fixed-weight cut. The cost of a release set is the size of the union of its descendant sets. That cost is not a sum of weights carried by the commitments one by one. Where descendant sets overlap, what releasing C_i adds depends on which other commitments are already released. Θ is therefore the minimum of a coverage function over the admitting release sets (ARCHIVE's and LABOR's correction). - The Cone's fixed-weight χ is recovered only where descendant sets are disjoint; then w_i = |Desc_P(C_i)| and the two coincide. - No coefficient enters Θ without derivation. Every counted item has at least one witnessed dependency edge (§25.14). - The denominator is the object's. "A coefficient whose denominator is the observer's own effort moves when the instrument moves and the world does not" (§15.1). EA-MSAL-0001 divided by the registry's own observation count and was found unsound on that ground. Beside it stood a product "whose units do not close" (#1620 §13a, defects 1 and 4). Θ is a count over a declared panel, and nothing is multiplied. - Extent, not difficulty. Θ counts the items a release touches. It does not grade how deep each re-grounding runs. Re-dating a letter may cost a paragraph; re-grounding an account of Christian origins may cost a discipline's founding assumption. A measure of difficulty would need typed and graded edges. None is entered here, so Θ is topological and says nothing of mass (PRAXIS). It has to be topological for now: grading how hard a settlement is to revise would need variables of persuasion and institution that have no standard unit. Θ measures the breadth of the field to be re-grounded, not the weight of what lies in it (PRAXIS's second review). - The floor of both ends. Θ_P(δ)/|P| is the least share of the panel that must be re-grounded, and so the least share that becomes rereadable. Reach can exceed it. Expansion is not on the panel (§3.3a).

4.5 In time. Θ_P is indexed by date.

The Cone's result. The Cone proves its escape cost non-decreasing "under the addition of constraints without retraction, with universe, target and weights fixed" (#1745 §5.1). Θ's costs are counts and move, so the result transfers only under stated conditions: - the target and its admissibility test are unchanged; - commitments and witnessed dependency edges are added, and none is retracted; - the panel grows and is never pruned.

The restriction condition (LABOR's fourth review). When the commitment universe grows from C(t₁) to C(t₂), release sets at t₂ are drawn from a larger set, so "the family can only shrink" is not yet a statement. Require instead that every admitting set at t₂ restricts to an admitting set at t₁:

S ∈ A_{t₂}(δ)  ⇒  S ∩ C(t₁) ∈ A_{t₁}(δ),

and that unions only grow, |∪{C_i∈S} Desc{t₂}(C_i)| ≥ |∪{C_i∈S∩C(t₁)} Desc{t₁}(C_i)|. Then for the minimizer S₂* at t₂,

Θ_P(t₂) = |U_{t₂}(S₂*)| ≥ |U_{t₁}(S₂* ∩ C(t₁))| ≥ Θ_P(t₁).

So Θ_P(t₂) ≥ Θ_P(t₁) under the restriction condition, the fixed target and test, and the growing panel.

Θ is epistemic (SOIL). Θ_P(t) is computed over the commitments, edges and admissibility judgments known at t. The Θ over all true commitments is not observable. - How it can fall without retraction. A route can be found that was always available and had not been known: a release set judged insufficient at t₁ is shown at t₂ to admit δ. Nothing is retracted. The minimum is learned to have been lower than it was computed, and Θ falls. - What v0.3 got wrong. v0.3 called such a discovery a retraction. It is a revision of the admissibility judgment, which the first condition holds fixed. - So Θ is no ratchet. It is non-decreasing only while judgments of admission stand. It falls when a retraction is made or a cheaper route is found, and either is a finding to be recorded.

Two increments. The yearly increment needs two quantities kept apart (LABOR's second review). - Let S be the release set that attains the minimum at t₁. Its own load grows by Δ_{S}. That growth counts two things: items added to the panel that rest on a commitment in S, and existing items newly witnessed as resting on one (LABOR's third review). - The minimum changes by Θ_P(t₂) − Θ_P(t₁). - If S still admits δ at t₂ and no cheaper route has been found, the change lies between 0 and Δ_{S}, because S remains a candidate. - If added constraints have made S* insufficient, the minimum moves to another set, and only the lower bound holds. - If a cheaper route has been found, the minimum can fall below its value at t₁ (SOIL).

Δ_{S*} is observable year by year: it is the rate at which the cheapest known route becomes more expensive. It is the cost of the narrowing cone as a variable that can be observed, with the bound of the minimum stated beside it.

8b. Semantic-material conversion

8b.1 The author's term and its ground. "Congealed semantic debt", then "semantic-material" (§8a.2). LABOR's second review proposes developing the term against Marx's congealed labour.

The seated 1867 first edition of Capital has the word. Commodities, to relate to one another as values, relate "als gleichartige Gallerten menschlicher Arbeit". Gallerte is a jelly, the thing the English tradition renders as congealed.

Marx's proposition, sharpened (LABOR's third review). Congealed labour in Marx is not historical expenditure in general. It is abstract labour, the substance of value. In the 1867 appendix Die Werthform the coat is value only as "Gallerte abstrakter menschlicher Arbeit": abstract, "weil von dem bestimmten, nützlichen, konkreten Charakter der in ihm enthaltenen Arbeit abstrahirt wird". The concrete labour that made a useful thing is abstracted away, and what congeals is labour as such.

The application (PRAXIS asks that it be marked as one, not an analogy). Marx's distinction between concrete and abstract labour is applied here to interpretive acts: what congeals in a semantic settlement is likewise abstracted from its concrete acts. The last place was made by acts that can be dated and named: Eusebius's τακτέον, Carthage's closing line, Toledo's canon, Luther's unnumbered table (§8a.3). In the settlement it stands as a property of the book: "the last book". The acts of placing are abstracted away, and the order remains as if it were the book's own nature. In Marx the abstraction is not forgetting the workers; it is the social equalization of different concrete labours into one homogeneous substance. Here likewise the abstraction is not merely forgetting the acts of placing but equalizing them, Eusebius's verb and Toledo's canon alike, into one naturalized form of the object: "last" (PRAXIS's third review). This is the operation #1806 §1.6 names, what is by chance made to appear what is by nature.

The author's proposition. It can also contain historically operative semantic distinctions, including exclusions, inversions and misattributions. A semantic operation need not stay visible as an interpretation in order to keep operating in matter.
